# Why this screen — the display as the home's honest instrument

The pitch in one breath: **a screen you glance at the way you glance at a
good watch** — it tells you the house is fine without asking for your
attention, it can *prove* what it says, and every sensor you add makes it
smarter. Open like the 3D-printer world, not walled like a garden:
anything that speaks the fleet's language (MQTT topics + the signed-chain
grammar + the SCV1 QR) can join, from our canaries or anyone else's.

## What already makes it self-evident

- **The living canary** — system health you read as a feeling, honestly
  (every pose maps to a log line). Nobody else's status display is loved.
- **Scan-to-join** — the display *is* the commissioner: show a code,
  point a new canary at it, hear the chirp. Wyze-slick, CVE-free.
- **The glass in your pocket** — the display serves its own live mirror,
  3D model, help, master settings, device receipt, and a browser serial
  monitor. Bench triage and grandma's brightness slider, same page.
- **Proof, not promises** — signed chains, verified badges, a witness
  record that holds up.

## The flagship next moves (in order of wow-per-week)

1. **Find my things.** The WAP canaries already run a Bluetooth Scout
   that hears beacons, tags and phones with per-canary signal strength.
   Surface it: the mirror (and the glass) answers "where are my keys?"
   with "near the Kitchen canary, strong." No new hardware — the fleet
   IS the finder mesh, and each added canary tightens the fix.
2. **Room rhythms.** Presence trends per room from CSI/radar witnesses —
   the display shows a day that *looks different* before it becomes a
   problem. (Care wave already has the per-witness rhythm baseline.)
3. **Time from the sky.** A GPS canary gossips constellation time to the
   fleet — the display cross-checks NTP+NIST against it and the house
   keeps honest time with zero internet.
4. **More senses, same glass.** Radar (canary-sense), acoustic events,
   air quality — new witnesses appear on the mirror the moment they join.
   The peripheral story compounds: the screen you bought gets better
   every time anyone ships a new canary.

## The rule that keeps it ours

Everything above stays local, provable and consent-first: no cloud
dependency, no phone-home, physical-proximity onboarding, and the
transparency sheet stays honest about every sense the house has.
