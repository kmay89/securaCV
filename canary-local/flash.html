Flash a Canary, right here

“Flashing” is just the word for loading firmware — the board's built-in software — onto it. First-time setup of a brand-new Canary or an update later: same two clicks, right here in the browser. No Arduino, no PlatformIO, no terminal. It backs the board up by itself, installs a signed image, and checks every byte against the chip. Built so you can't pick the wrong firmware, and can't brick the board. It's a workbench too: run a health check or open a serial monitor without leaving the page.

Honest by construction: the product list and each board's chip are generated from the firmware tree by tools/gen_flash.py and drift-checked in CI, so a board is only ever offered an image built for its own silicon. Firmware images come from the project's signed GitHub releases; the flasher verifies each one's SHA-256 before writing and the device's own MD5 after. The flashing engine is esptool-js, vendored into this site — not loaded from a CDN. Nothing about your board leaves this page.
