The Canary Nursery — where Canaries hatch

Every Canary starts here: plug a bare board in and it leaves with a name, a voice, and a job. “Flashing” is just the word for loading firmware — the board's built-in software — and the Nursery does it in two clicks, right in the browser. No Arduino, no PlatformIO, no terminal. It backs the board up by itself, installs a signed image, checks every byte against the chip, bakes in your WiFi and room dials, and then makes the newborn prove itself — live. Built so you can't pick the wrong firmware, and can't brick the board. Raising a whole batch? The Nursery keeps the roster so you never lose your place.

Honest by construction: the product list and each board's chip are generated from the firmware tree by tools/gen_flash.py and drift-checked in CI, so a board is only ever offered an image built for its own silicon. Firmware images come from the project's signed GitHub releases; the flasher verifies each one's SHA-256 before writing and the device's own MD5 after. The flashing engine is esptool-js, vendored into this site — not loaded from a CDN. Nothing about your board leaves this page.
