The Proving Ground

You flashed the radar — and it went silent. This is its voice: plug the Sense in over USB and the bench hears every line it speaks, turns every knob it holds, and walks you from “it flashed” to “it works, calibrated, in the right spot.” No board on the cable? Boot the twin — the firmware's own pipeline, ported line-for-line, speaking the identical console dialect — and run the same drills to see exactly what passing looks like.

Honest by construction: the twin generates real MR60 wire bytes and runs them through assets/sense-sim.js — the line-for-line port of the firmware's parser and FSMs that CI pins against firmware/tests_host/ — and its console is a port of firmware/common/console/tuning_console.h, drift-gated so it can only ever speak the dialect the real cable speaks. Every threshold, bound, placement figure and knob description here is parsed from the firmware and docs/hardware/mr60bha2_radar_notes.md, never written twice. Raw centimeters and BPM appear only under the explicit bench-detail switch, only on this attended cable — the same privacy exception the firmware documents.
