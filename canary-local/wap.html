The WAP — first boot

The Canary WAP feels presence through the WiFi field itself — no camera. And it's literally a WiFi Access Point: power it on and it hands you a network to set it up from. Meet it here before you own one — the real board, its own boot log, the captive-portal wizard, and the MQTT it speaks.
