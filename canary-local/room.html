Canary Lab

A workshop you can walk

Every tool you built, in one warm room. One straight line, stage 1 to 6 — tap any machine to open its bench, or walk the whole line.

  • A room, not a menu. Each machine looks like the thing it stands for — you can't get lost in a place.
  • Options, not forks. At stage 4 you pick the sense right at the bench: camera or radar today, more senses will dock there.
  • Day & night. Same room, two moods. On a phone it folds back to the vertical build line.

Prototype — hand-built iso models; links open the live benches. An homage to the isometric rooms on kmay89.com & dmeves.com.
