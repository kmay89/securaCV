Listen for a smoke alarm

A Canary WAP can hear a smoke or CO alarm and pass it to your Home Assistant — not by recording anything, but by recognizing the cadence every UL-listed alarm sounds. This bench runs that exact detector — the real firmware, compiled to WebAssembly — on your microphone, so you can watch it work before you own a board. Press your alarm's TEST button nearby and see the same code the device runs light up, beep by beep.

← the Lab · flash it in your browser · the WAP's guided first boot · the Dash's mic (4.3C) · the Hub · works offline · nothing here phones anywhere

Honest by construction: the tone-gate frequency, envelope thresholds and T3/T4 cadence targets shown here are read from the running detector, and the verdict — is this beep alarm-band, does this rhythm fit — is made by firmware/projects/canary-wap/…/securacv_audio.cpp compiled to WebAssembly, the same source a host test drives with synthesized audio and CI rebuilds and byte-diffs. Your microphone is the only new input; its audio is processed frame by frame in this page and never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. This is a demonstration of how the detector behaves — not a smoke or CO alarm, and no substitute for a UL-listed one.
