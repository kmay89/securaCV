The Vault

Sometimes the rare thing matters — a smoke alarm at 3 a.m., a frame worth keeping. SecuraCV's answer is a vault: evidence sealed so the device can't read it back, that no one can open alone — it takes a quorum. Here's what those three words mean, with the code behind them.
