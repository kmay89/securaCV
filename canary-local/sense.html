The Sense — radar school

The Canary Sense witnesses presence over 60 GHz radar — no camera, no microphone, no idea who you are. It knows someone is there, how many (0/1/2+), how far (near/mid/far), and — bedside, opted-in — that they're breathing. Meet it here: the real cone, the real thresholds, the real boot log, and the placement playbook that gets the most out of it with the fewest false alarms.

Honest by construction: every threshold, topic, boot line, LED color and HA entity is parsed from firmware/projects/canary-sense and docs/canary_sense_mr60bha2_design.md by tools/gen_sense.py, and drift-checked in CI.
