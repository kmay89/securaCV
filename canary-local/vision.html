The Vision — first watch

The Canary Vision detects people on the camera module's own chip — an NPU behind the lens — and only someone is here ever leaves the board. Meet it here before you own one: the module in 3D, the two-port gotcha, the one-time model load with its bounding-box preview, the boot log, the MQTT it speaks, and the boxes-only way you aim it forever after.

Honest by construction: the thresholds, topics, entities, boot lines, port rules and walkthrough steps are parsed from firmware/projects/canary-vision and docs/hardware/ by tools/gen_vision.py, and drift-checked in CI.
