    Build a Hub
    
            
    Turn a card into a Home Assistant hub
    
            
    The brain your Canaries report home to. Only external, removable
              disks are ever offered — and the write runs only after you type ERASE.
    
          
    

          
          
    
            
            Picking up where you left off — still watching for the hub you just made…
            
            
          
    

          
    
            
    1
    What you're making
    
            
    A Home Assistant hub for your Canaries: we download the
              official Home Assistant OS image,
              verify it , write it to your card, read every byte
              back to prove the write, and seed your Wi-Fi so the Pi joins your network on
              its own. 
    
            
    
            
    
              Have a different Pi?
              
    
            
    
            
    
              What happens on first boot?
              
    Home Assistant OS keeps itself updated and heals itself (A/B rollback if an
                update ever goes wrong). First boot takes 10–20 minutes while it sets up —
                then it appears on your network as
                homeassistant.local:8123.
    
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    2
    Plug in your card or SSD
    
            
    Insert a microSD card (or plug in a USB SSD — the durable
              choice for a Pi 5). It's picked up automatically.
    
            
    
            
    
              No card reader? Use the Pi itself over USB-C
              
    Your Raspberry Pi 5 can be its own card reader — we borrow it for a
                minute and it shows up here as a disk (SD card and NVMe).
                The cable powers it; no power supply needed.
    
              
      
                
    1. Leave the Pi unpowered. Put the card you want to flash in it (or leave the NVMe in).

    3
    Your Wi-Fi & account
    
            
    All written onto the card only — never logged, never sent anywhere.
              The hub joins your Wi-Fi the moment it boots.
    
            
    
              
              
            
    
            
    
              
              
              
            
    

            
    
              Skip Home Assistant's setup wizard — pre-make your account
                experimental
              
    Type your login now and first visit to the hub is a
                login page, not a setup wizard. Your password is scrambled
                on this computer and only the scrambled form goes on the card.
    
              
    
                
                
                
                
              
    
              
    
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
    4
    Write it
    
            
    
            
    

            
    
              ✓
              Nothing to worry about here. We only ever write to the card you picked —
                your computer's own drives are never even offered — and we read every byte back to prove it
                worked. You can stop at any moment; the card just needs a fresh flash if you do. Even a
                counterfeit card that lies about the write gets caught right here, at your desk.
            
    

            
    To be sure, type ERASE —
              everything on the chosen card will be replaced.
