# Code of Conduct

SecuraCV is a project about protecting people who can't always protect
themselves. That value applies to this community, not just the product.

## The short version

- **Be kind, be specific, assume good faith.** Critique code and ideas, never
  the person.
- **Privacy is the point.** Don't post other people's personal information,
  footage, home addresses, or device locations — not in issues, PRs, the
  gallery, or discussions. Redact before you share.
- **No harassment.** No demeaning, discriminatory, or sexual language or
  imagery. No sustained disruption. No intimidation, public or private.
- **Reviews are adversarial by design; reviewers are not.** We interrogate
  failure modes and future misuse hard (see the review philosophy in
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md)). That scrutiny is aimed at the threat
  model, never at the contributor.
- **Don't weaponize the project.** Using SecuraCV, or this community, to
  surveil or track people against their consent is contrary to everything the
  project stands for and is not welcome here.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, the community gallery — and when you're representing the project
in public.

## Reporting

- **Security-sensitive or privacy-sensitive reports** (including a conduct
  issue that involves someone's personal data): use the private channel in
  [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) or a GitHub private security advisory. Do not
  open a public issue.
- **Everything else:** email the maintainers privately at
  **errerlabs@gmail.com** (the same address [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)
  publishes), or open a GitHub issue if the report is fine being public.
  A conduct report about a person never has to go through a public issue.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who cannot review a report
impartially will recuse themselves.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this code,
and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban anyone whose behavior
they judge harmful — with a private explanation where possible. We favor
repair over punishment, but the safety of the community and of the people the
project exists to protect comes first.

---

This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor
Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), tuned to a privacy project.
