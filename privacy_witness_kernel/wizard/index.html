SecuraCV

A security camera with a 24-hour memory.
Clips stay on your hardware. They auto-delete. The only thing that persists is a tamper-proof log that proves nobody altered the record.

What this wizard does: Connects your cameras to Frigate, configures the Privacy Witness Kernel, and sets up daily notifications — in about 2 minutes.
