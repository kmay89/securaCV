SecuraCV

The verified witness log for Frigate.
Frigate keeps the video; SecuraCV keeps the proof: every event signed into a tamper-evident chain, never faces, plates or footage.

What this wizard does: Connects SecuraCV to your Frigate and Mosquitto, chooses what to seal and for how long, and sets up the daily digest — about two minutes, usually nothing to type.

📷 Frigate's cameras

    SecuraCV seals every person, car, dog and cat event Frigate reports on these cameras. Faces, plates and names Frigate recognizes are dropped before anything is written. Cameras are named by place, not by person — keep it that way in Frigate.

    📷 Add Cameras

    No running Frigate was found, so SecuraCV writes Frigate's camera config for you. Enter the name and RTSP stream URL for each camera. Name cameras by place ("Front door", "Driveway"), not by person.

    ⚙️ Settings

    The witness log is tiny (coarse claims, never video). Exports are self-contained, so you lose nothing by exporting first. Frigate's own clip retention is Frigate's setting, untouched here.
