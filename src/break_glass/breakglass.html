What happens when you break the glass

Sealed evidence stays encrypted until enough of your chosen trustees agree to open it. This console walks you through the four steps above. It's hard to get wrong, and safe to try:

  • Nothing here can delete or alter the record — opening the vault only adds to the log.
  • It can't happen silently: the unseal itself is written into the permanent, tamper-evident record.
  • No one person — including you — can open the vault alone. That's the point.
  • Changed your mind? Closing a request just discards the approvals; the evidence stays sealed.

Access is quorum-gated per Invariant V. Every unseal decision (granted or denied) is recorded as an Ed25519-signed break-glass receipt, hash-chained into the sealed log and visible to anyone who later verifies the record. Opening, approving, or closing a request keeps state in the server session only — the permanent record is written at the unseal decision.

1 · Connect

Paste the capability token from the machine that runs securaCV. It proves to the server that you're allowed to ask.

Read it from the server's token file. It stays in this tab and is sent only to this server, as a Bearer credential.

2 · Open an unlock request

Name the sealed envelope, say why you need it, and say who you are. If the vault is opened, your name and the reason you give are written into the permanent record — write them the way you'd want them read later.

Produces the SHA-256 request hash that every trustee signs; approvals are bound to this exact envelope, purpose, and requester, and can't be replayed elsewhere.

Opens a signer-only page (no token needed, makes no server calls): the trustee signs in their own browser and sends you back the signature to paste in step 3. Works even if the trustee can't reach this server.

3 · Collect approvals

Each trustee reviews the request and sends you back a signature. Paste each one below — they'll turn green as they're verified and counted.

Trustees sign the request hash with their own key (offline via break_glass approve, or the share link's in-browser signer). The server verifies each Ed25519 signature against the trustee's registered public key before counting it.

4 · Quorum & unseal

When enough trustees have signed, the Unseal button lights up. Opening the vault also writes a receipt of who approved it and why — that's what keeps the record trustworthy.

Unsealed evidence is written to the server's output directory, never returned over the network. A signed break-glass receipt is appended to the log whether access is granted or denied.
